We're thrilled to announce that
[ActivityPub](https://www.w3.org/TR/activitypub/)
has officially reached
[Candidate Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/2005/10/Process-20051014/tr.html#RecsCR)
status!
This is a huge milestone, signaling that ActivityPub has moved to a
point of stability where we're no longer planning major revisions
and would like to see as many projects move to implement as possible.

In other words, now's a great time for *YOU* to start implementing!
Head on over to
[the standard](https://www.w3.org/TR/activitypub/)
and start building!

We're also planning tools to
~~test~~ your implementations soon, and
~~submit implementation reports~~ (ed: obsolete and removed in 2026).
There's a lot to do, but we're on track towards being a proposed
recommendation to the W3C!
