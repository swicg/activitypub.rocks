Hi all, this is [Johannes Ernst](https://j12t.org/), in my role as the lead
of the website task force of the
[Social Web Community Group](https://www.w3.org/community/socialcg/)
in the [W3C](https://www.w3.org/).

As Christine just announced in another post, she is stepping down as the maintainer of
this site. Maintenance of activitypub.rocks is passing to SWICG.

We are just starting, but we have a plan! Please refer to the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub.rocks/issues)
and the [README.md](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub.rocks/blob/main/README.md)
in the repo for this site.

Want to help? PRs are appreciated. And, of course, you are welcome to join SWICG and help out.
