A little over one year later after
[reaching Candidate Recommendation status](/news/activitypub-reaches-cr/)
we're pleased to announce that
[ActivityPub](https://www.w3.org/TR/activitypub/)
has now reached
[Proposed Recommendation status](https://www.w3.org/2005/10/Process-20051014/tr.html#RecsPR)!
(We actually moved to Proposed Recommendation status
[on Tuesday](https://www.w3.org/TR/2017/PR-activitypub-20171205/)
but... sometimes getting out news is a slow process.)
This means that as far as the
[Social Working Group](https://www.w3.org/wiki/Socialwg/)
is concerned, the group is confident that ActivityPub is ready to
become a real specification.
Now we wait and cross our fingers for the W3C Membership and W3C
Management to approve.
Assuming all goes well, that would mean moving to
[Recommendation status](https://www.w3.org/2005/10/Process-20051014/tr.html#RecsPR)
sometime over the next couple of months.

In the meanwhile we're seeing the number of
~~implementations of ActivityPub to be growing~~ (ed: obsolete and removed in 2026).
What exciting new ActivityPub-using projects are on the horizon?
We hope to see you submit your implementation to the implementation
reports page next!
